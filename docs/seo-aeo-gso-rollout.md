# Local SEO, AEO and AI-search rollout checklist — Jay's Land Clearing

## Implemented in this change
- 12 dedicated service-detail pages with original service-specific planning guidance, estimate considerations, two visible Q&As and relevant internal links.
- /services directory with clear entry links to all 12.
- Links from homepage service cards, every location page, header and footer to the appropriate service information.
- Sitemap entries for the directory and all 12 detail pages; existing location and blog sitemap entries are retained.
- Individual titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Service structured data and BreadcrumbList structured data.
- Removed unconfirmed hours and priceRange from sitewide structured data.

## Human verification required (do not invent)
- Verify all 12 services really are provided and specify scope limits, especially demolition, hoarding cleanouts and hazardous material handling.
- Verify actual operating hours and the homepage/footer claim of 15+ years of experience.
- Confirm service-area radius and availability in each listed city.
- Check Google Business Profile name, phone, URL, services, categories and business hours against actual records; correct inconsistencies.
- Gather approved original project photos: before/after, approximate acreage or scope, community, equipment, outcome, permission to publish, and job date.
- Gather a real client testimonial and permission for each case study; don't invent ratings or review quotes.
- Confirm permits / licensing / insurance statements before publishing any.
- Verify the official website in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit https://jayslandclearingserviceanddirtwork.com/sitemap.xml.
- Validate 200 responses, crawl status, canonical selection, robots rules, mobile rendering, and rich data output for /services, all 12 service pages, representative city pages.
- Verify GA4/GTM, Vercel Analytics and conversion tracking for calls, SMS clicks and both quote forms.

## Recommended project-case-study template
- Headline: [type of work] in [city, TX] — [approximate area]
- Job context: [property type; client's goal]
- Starting condition: [vegetation, terrain, debris, access]
- Scope performed: [work actually completed; equipment]
- Challenges / safety: [if permitted to share]
- Result: [specific evidence-backed outcome, not guarantees]
- Real before/after media: [approved photos and alt text]
- Quote CTA: [phone and estimate link]

## Measurement plan (monthly)
- Search Console: indexed service pages, impressions, clicks and target queries (split branded / nonbranded).
- Bing Webmaster Tools: indexing and supported AI citation reporting.
- Business Profile: calls, direction requests and website interactions, if available.
- Website: call-button, text-button and form events; reconcile with real customer inquiries.
- Check external AI-search referrals and citation samples, but do not report invented ranks or guaranteed AI inclusion.

Note: Google Search guidance says there are no special requirements to appear in AI Overviews beyond standard indexing and search eligibility, and no need for special AI text files.
